OPEN TASK PRINCIPLES

Work should be clear and fair

  • Workers are never charged to register, view tasks, apply, unlock wages, or receive payment.
  • Agree on deliverables, deadline, budget, currency, revisions, and payment terms before work starts.
  • Do not share passwords, OTPs, banking credentials, seed phrases, or unnecessary identity documents.
  • Be cautious with suspicious links, downloads, pressure tactics, and requests to move off-platform before terms are clear.
  • Keep contact information private until both parties explicitly consent to an introduction.
Important limitation: This prototype does not verify employers, process payments, hold funds, provide escrow, guarantee wages, or resolve disputes. Any future live version needs moderation, privacy, security, legal, and operational review.

Pay for work. Not idle capacity.

Employers can define a task and budget around a real need. Workers can choose opportunities that fit their skills and availability. Fair pay and clear scope matter, including for small tasks.
